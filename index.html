syamoji

syamoji is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to manage their own social media content.

With TikTok integration, the account owner can sign in with TikTok and upload their own videos to their TikTok account as drafts. The owner then reviews, edits, and publishes each draft manually in the TikTok app.

Features

  • Sign in with TikTok (Login Kit)
  • Upload the owner's own videos to TikTok drafts (Content Posting API)
  • No automatic publishing — every post is reviewed by the owner
