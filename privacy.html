Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Information We Access

When you sign in with TikTok, the Service receives your basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) and an access token, according to the permissions you grant.

2. How We Use It

This information is used only to identify your account and to upload videos you select to your TikTok drafts.

3. Storage

Access tokens and profile information are stored locally on the owner's own device. They are not sent to any third-party server other than TikTok.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can revoke the Service's access at any time from your TikTok app settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services). Locally stored data can be deleted by signing out of the Service.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
